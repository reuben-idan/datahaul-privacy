Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025 | Effective: May 2025

1. Introduction

DataHaul Inc. ("we", "our", "us") operates the DataHaul mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App and the choices you have associated with that data.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Email address, username, phone number, and account type (Farmer, Supplier, Buyer) when you create an account.
  • Location Data: With your explicit permission, we access your device location to provide weather forecasts and local market prices relevant to your area.
  • Camera & Photos: With your explicit permission, we access your camera and photo library for profile pictures and community posts.
  • Transaction Data: Products purchased, delivery addresses, payment references, and order history.
  • Usage Data: Products viewed, searches performed, and features used to improve recommendations and app experience.
  • Traceability Data: Crop type, harvest dates, farming practices, and sustainability scores for produce traceability features.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain our agricultural marketplace service
  • To process orders and payments via Paystack
  • To provide AI-powered crop recommendations and market intelligence
  • To deliver weather-based farming advice for your location
  • To enable community features (posts, groups, comments)
  • To send order confirmations, delivery updates, and important notifications
  • To improve our services, personalize your experience, and provide relevant product recommendations
  • To facilitate produce traceability and export marketplace transactions

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely on our servers hosted on Amazon Web Services (AWS). Authentication tokens are stored in encrypted secure storage on your device. All data transmission uses HTTPS encryption. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Paystack: Payment processing (card, mobile money, bank transfer). Subject to Paystack's Privacy Policy.
  • OpenWeatherMap: Weather data for your farming location.
  • Amazon Web Services (AWS): Cloud hosting and AI services for crop recommendations and market intelligence.
  • Expo (EAS): Push notification delivery and app updates.

6. Permissions

  • Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): Used for weather forecasts and local market data. Only accessed with your explicit permission. You can revoke this at any time in device settings.
  • Camera (CAMERA): Used for taking profile photos and community post images. Only accessed when you explicitly choose to take a photo.
  • Storage (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Used for selecting photos from your gallery for profile and posts.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE): Used for haptic feedback on interactions.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data to third parties. We share data only:

  • With payment processors (Paystack) to complete transactions
  • With sellers/buyers as necessary to fulfill marketplace orders
  • With delivery partners to fulfill physical product orders
  • As required by applicable law or legal process

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to provide our services and fulfill the purposes described in this policy. Order history is retained for record-keeping. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data through your profile settings
  • Update or correct your information at any time
  • Request deletion of your account and data by contacting support
  • Revoke location, camera, or storage permissions in your device settings
  • Opt out of non-essential notifications

10. Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.
